The Best Version of You

Embarking on a Voyage to a Better Self — by Anagha S Setlur

For those who wish to kindle their latent selves and take that one step further to becoming something you have always dreamed of, this journey of a book is for you. Insights in various areas of self-development and character building, with easily understandable pointers at the end of every chapter, this narrative unfolds the realities that seeks out. Unravel the potential that lurks within you from the various tips and suggestions provided and embark on an expedition of your own towards self-discovery, betterment, peace and clarity. A ride that we all will take together, this book showcases the realities, not only of the world outside but of the deep, roiling, chaotic realm within each and every one of us, ever ready to be unleashed and churned.

The simple ideas and humble advices provided here may help reach and inspire one of you on a similar journey to help gear up for happiness, joy, abundance and to feeling like oneself again.
