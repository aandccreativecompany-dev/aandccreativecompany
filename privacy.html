Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026. This covers aandccreativeventures.netlify.app and the Prakriyā app.

A&C Creative Ventures ("we", "us") runs this website and the Prakriyā app. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email us at the address at the bottom of this page.

What we collect on this website

If you submit the masterclass registration form or the newsletter signup, we collect the name, email, and any other details the form asks for, so we can register you and send you what you signed up for.

This site runs Google AdSense, which may use cookies to show ads and can collect browsing data for that purpose. It's built and hosted on Netlify, which processes standard technical/server data (like IP address and request logs) to serve the site.

What Prakriyā collects

Prakriyā works fully offline by default. Your habits, goals, journal entries, mood logs, nutrition entries, and other in-app content are stored locally on your device unless you turn on cloud backup.

If you sign in with Google (optional, for cloud backup): we store your app content in Firebase/Firestore under your Google account, so it can sync across devices and survive a reinstall. We receive your name and email from Google as part of sign-in.

Biometric unlock (optional, e.g. fingerprint/face unlock for the app): this is handled entirely by your device's own operating system. We never see or store your biometric data.

Nutrition & food logging: meal entries you log are stored locally (and, if cloud backup is on, in Firestore as above). We don't analyze this data ourselves beyond showing it back to you in the app, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Coach chat: if you use the in-app AI coach, your messages are sent — through our own server-side proxy, never with your name or account details attached — to Anthropic's API to generate a reply. The coach has no access to your habit, mood, journal, or nutrition data; every conversation starts fresh. We don't store your chat history on our servers — it stays on your device, same as the rest of your app data.

Notifications: reminder times you set are stored locally to schedule your notifications. We don't collect this data ourselves.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't share your app content (habits, journal, mood, nutrition entries) with advertisers. We don't use your coach chat messages to train any model.

Your choices

You can use Prakriyā without ever signing in — cloud backup and coach chat are both optional. You can delete your local app data at any time from your device's app settings. If you've signed in with Google and want your Firestore data deleted, email us and we'll remove it.

Third parties we use

Google (Sign-In, Firebase/Firestore, AdSense), Netlify (site and function hosting), and Anthropic (the AI model behind coach chat, called only through our own proxy — see above). Each handles data under their own privacy policy in addition to this one.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
