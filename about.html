About Us

Growth mindset, made practical.

We exist to cultivate a global growth mindset — turning outcome engineering principles and mindset theory into daily habits people can actually act on.

A&C Creative Ventures started as a simple idea: outcome engineering and productivity are usually taught as opposites — one all mindset, no action; the other all action, no meaning. We wanted to build the space in between.

Every video, post, and piece of content we make is built around one question: what's the one specific, practical step someone can take today? Not vague inspiration — a real shift they can make before they close the tab.

Our content lives primarily on YouTube (long-form breakdowns and Shorts), Instagram (daily carousels and reels), and Pinterest — and this site pulls it all together in one place, alongside the Outcome Engineering Planner & Tracker and Prakriyā, the daily practice app we're building for anyone who wants to take it further.
